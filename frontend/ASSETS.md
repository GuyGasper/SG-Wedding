# Homepage assets

`public/images/engagement-on-the-steps.jpg` is the supplied black-and-white photograph, encoded at 2400 × 1600 for the web. The full 3:2 frame is displayed at 80% of the available photo width on desktop and mobile.

[Cormorant Garamond Medium](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond), Pinyon Script, and [Jost](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jost) are self-hosted Google Fonts. They approximate the reference site’s display serif, script, and geometric sans serif. Their SIL Open Font License files are included in `public/fonts/`. The floral ornament is original inline SVG.

The footer credit uses the Minted wordmark from the [reference wedding site](https://demos.oursite.minted.com/MIN-QJ6-DWW/3812340/l1), stored locally as public/images/minted-wordmark-white.svg and linked to Minted.
